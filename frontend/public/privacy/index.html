Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy for SmartSpend

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 • Application: SmartSpend (https://smart-spend-umber.vercel.app)

Welcome to SmartSpend. Your privacy and trust are paramount. This Privacy Policy details how SmartSpend collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our web application, particularly through Google Sign-In and our financial tracking services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or interact with SmartSpend, we collect the following:

  • Google Account Information (OAuth): When you sign in using Google, we access your basic profile information authorized by you, including your full name, email address, and profile picture avatar.
  • Financial Records: Transaction details (income and expenses), amounts, categories, dates, payment methods, planning lists, and currencies that you actively input into SmartSpend.
  • Preferences: Display currency preferences, date formatting choices, and notification settings.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

SmartSpend adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only use Google user data to authenticate your account and personalize your expense tracking dashboard.
  • We NEVER sell Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • We NEVER use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements.
  • No human reads your private financial data or profile data unless you give explicit permission for troubleshooting, it is required by law, or it is completely anonymized.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To establish and authenticate your SmartSpend account.
  • To calculate monthly totals, balances, category charts, and budget analytics.
  • To synchronize planned expenses and purchases across your devices.
  • To secure our infrastructure, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure account safety.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Your financial transactions and authentication tokens are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS) and stored in secure PostgreSQL databases powered by Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled. Only your authenticated user account can access or modify your records.

Data is retained as long as your account exists. If you choose to delete your account, all associated financial records and personal data are permanently deleted.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can access, edit, or permanently delete your account and all associated transactions at any time in the app settings, or by emailing our support team.
